Miller Richters, a third-year graduate student in the Griffith lab, was awarded a Scholar-in-Training Award for the 2020 AACR Annual Meeting. This award is funded by the American Brain Tumor Association for Miller's research on personalized neoantigen vaccines for glioblastoma multiforme patients. Their abstract is entitled "Molecular heterogeneity in glioblastoma multiforme influences variant clonality and neoantigen prediction accuracy."
