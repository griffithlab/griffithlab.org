We are pleased to announce that Susanna Kiwala, a software developer in the Griffith Lab, has been awarded an R50 Research Software Engineer grant by the National Cancer Institute (NCI). This award (R50CA305826) will provide support for Susanna’s continued work on developing the pVACtools software platform over the next several years.
