CIViC has been named in a [list of 37 Global Core Biodata Resources](https://globalbiodata.org/what-we-do/global-core-biodata-resources/list-of-current-global-core-biodata-resources) alongside other important resources such as Ensembl, ClinGen and Gnomad. The GCBR includes select resources that ensure the long term preservation of biological data, and are of fundamental importance to the biological and life sciences community.
